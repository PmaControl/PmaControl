While our engineering team is working on finalizing the TokuMXse storage engine, I want to provide an experimental build that you can try and test MongoDB 3.0 with our storage engine. It is available herepercona.com/downloads/TESTING/Percona-TokuMXse-rc5/percona-tokumxse-3.0.3pre-rc5.tar.gz To start MongoDB with TokuMXse storage engine use: mongod --storageEngine=tokuft <br> 1 mongod -- storageEngine = tokuft < br > I am looking for your feedback!
