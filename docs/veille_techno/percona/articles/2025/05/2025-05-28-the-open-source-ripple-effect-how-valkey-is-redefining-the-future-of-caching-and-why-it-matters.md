## Auteur source

Andrey Glazkov is a Product Manager at Percona, where he leads product strategy across Support, Professional Services, and Managed Services. While his primary focus is on these service pillars, he also spearheads initiatives in emerging technologies, notably Valkey (the open-source fork of Redis). Andrey has been instrumental in developing Percona’s enterprise-grade support for Valkey, aligning it with the company’s commitment to open-source excellence. He also played a key role in launching Percona’s EOL support for MySQL 5.7 EOL. Prior to Percona, Andrey drove LLM-powered product initiatives at PwC. He holds an MBA with a focus on Data Analytics from Arizona State University. Andrey combines technical fluency with go-to-market strategy to drive product innovation and customer success. He lives in Alexandria, VA.
